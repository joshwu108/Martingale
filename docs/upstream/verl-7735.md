# Draft comment for verl RFC #7735

Venue status (checked 2026-10-10): https://github.com/verl-project/verl/issues/7735 closed as completed on 2026-10-09 after the discussion narrowed to vLLM request hooks, merged in #8082. Keep this as an unposted draft for a future provenance discussion; #7735 is no longer a live venue. The following is a related schema suggestion, not a request to broaden that RFC's completed implementation.

The RFC's `logprobs_mode` payload field is useful: a sampled token's probability needs its provenance to be interpretable. For async rollouts, a compact companion record could carry, per sampled token, `(position, token_id, behavior_logprob_bits, revision_digest)`; the revision manifest binds the trainer step, parent revision, weights digest, tokenizer digest, and sampler settings including `logprobs_mode`. A score record then binds `(sequence_digest, position, train_revision_digest, trainer_logprob_bits)`. A per-token revision handles a generation that crosses a weight update; a per-sample version alone cannot express that. Section 7 of our [record design](https://github.com/joshwu108/Martingale/blob/main/docs/design.md) gives the exact fields and checker limits. This could remain optional metadata and would also help consumers of `selected_token_logprobs` know which distribution produced each value.

One concrete reason to keep engine and trainer log-probs separate: in a 16-step TRL 1.13/vLLM 0.28 colocated run, an fp32 trainer forward disagreed with its bf16 engine by 0.81 and 1.03 nats on generated tokens at two generations. We first misread it as tail noise, then as failed sync. An elementwise probe showed **zero of 494,032,768** engine parameter elements differed from the trainer cast to bf16 after each sync. The sync worked; most fp32 weight changes remained below a bf16 rounding boundary (12.3% of engine elements had changed in bf16 by step 4, 17.3% by step 12). With bf16 autocast, lag-0 mismatch was 0.0001–0.03 nats. [Probe and limits](https://github.com/joshwu108/Martingale/blob/main/docs/findings/2026-10-06-trl-colocate-sync.md).
