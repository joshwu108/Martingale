## T3 — Staleness Scaling Law

*(Protocol FROZEN 2026-08-31. Do not modify kill rules.)*

### MDP family

- Random rational MDPs
- |S| ∈ {3, 4, 5}
- |A| ∈ {2, 3}
- H ∈ {3, 4, 5}
- 20 MDPs per cell (|S|, |A|, H), drawn from a fixed campaign seed
  (to be committed here before any run)
- Campaign seed: `b"martingale-staleness-campaign-v1"` (frozen 2026-08-31)

### Revision chain

Policy drift generated by running exact rational gradient ascent steps
of rational learning rate `α = 1/10` (subject to sign-off).
Revision `r+k` is a deterministic exact function of revision `r`:
`π_{r+k}` = result of `k` exact gradient ascent steps from `π_r`.

### Measurement

For each (MDP, behavior_revision, target_revision, clip_ε) cell:
- Exact squared-L2 bias of clipped estimator (rational, no square roots)
- Exact squared gradient norm (rational)
- Exact relative squared bias = bias² / ‖∇J‖² (rational)

Lags k ∈ {0, 1, 2, 4, 8}.
Clip ε ∈ {1/10, 2/10, 3/10}.

### Kill rule

The **monotone-growth thesis** is dead in a cell if:
> exact relative squared bias at k=8 is **not** strictly greater than
> at k=1 for a **majority** (>50%) of that cell's MDPs.

The **global thesis** is dead if it dies in a majority of cells.

### Descriptive question (not a kill rule)

Report cells where the clipped estimator's exact bias is smaller than
the unclipped IS-REINFORCE estimator's exact error profile
(where "error profile" means the variance-induced deviation from the
exact gradient, computed by the same exact enumeration).
These must be reported fully, not buried.

## T4 — Float Divergence Study

*(Protocol FROZEN 2026-08-31. Do not modify kill rules.)*

### Float defendants

- float32 log-prob subtraction + exp + clip (numpy path)
- float64 log-prob subtraction + exp + clip (numpy path)
- float32 log-prob subtraction + exp + clip (torch path, in campaigns/float_baselines.py)

### Candidate construction

For each clip ε ∈ {1/10, 2/10}:
- Construct 10^5 (exact-ratio, parameterization) pairs designed to land within
  float32-ulp-scale distance of the clip boundary.
- Method: exact back-solving — choose exact ratios at distance j·ulp from the
  clip boundary for j ∈ {-4, -3, -2, -1, 0, 1, 2, 3, 4}, then find
  policy parameterizations (rational simplex entries) realizing those ratios.

Long-trajectory cumulative products:
- H ∈ {16, 64, 256} steps.
- Measure float underflow/overflow and float-vs-exact clip-activation
  disagreement.

### Measured outcomes

For each candidate:
- Exact IS ratio (Fraction)
- Float IS ratio (float32, float64)
- Which side of clip boundary each falls on
- Whether the gradient direction contribution flips

### Kill rule

Dead if **zero** boundary flips are found in the entire 10^5-candidate budget
per (ε, float-variant) combination.
(Boundary flips are near-certain to exist; the scientific content is their
frequency and magnitude structure.)

### Shrinkage

Every flip receives a shrunk minimal reproducer: the smallest rational
parameterization that still triggers the flip, found by automated bisection.
